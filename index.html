Product principles

Made to feel
obvious in use.

The visual language may change from app to app. The standard does not.

01

Useful first.

A clear job, solved directly. Features exist because they help, not because they fill a roadmap.

02

Calm by default.

Readable screens, predictable interactions, and as little friction as the task allows.

03

Respectful always.

Privacy, honest communication, and business choices that never compete with the product.
